How to play

🕹️Move with the joystick. Touch anywhere on the left half and it appears under your thumb.
🏓Hitting is automatic. Catch the ball on the edge of your paddle to angle it. Near the net you dink, from the back you drive.
🎮Controls (pause menu, tap to switch): AUTO is the default: you move with the joystick and your shot angle comes from where you catch the ball, with some randomness. MANUAL adds a pink radar that shows where your shot will land as the ball comes to you: move left or right of the ball to change it, and the faint circle is how far it can wobble. PRO has no joystick: a mini court sits bottom-left. Put your thumb on it and slide: wherever your thumb is on the mini court, your player runs to that spot on the real court (the pink dot is you; lift off and they stop). Tap anywhere on their side with your other thumb to choose where each return goes (tap the kitchen for a dink or drop, deep for a drive). Tap again or drag to change it before you hit. No tap means a normal auto shot. High CTL paddles land closer, and aiming right on the lines can go out. LOB, POWER and SPIN still work in every mode.
⚡LOB lofts your next shot. POWER rips a hard drive (riskier). SPIN curves and dips it, and kicks off the bounce. Paddles with a high SPN stat make it nastier; low-spin paddles make it weak and wild.
📏Rules: serve and return must bounce. No volleys from the kitchen, and if your follow-through after a volley carries you into the kitchen, that's a fault too. Only the server scores. Win by 2. Every game goes to 11. Easy is chill, Medium is faster, Hard is blazing fast.
✨Sprites float beside you in CPU matches. Each gives a passive boost and a power. Light powers (Ember, Tide, Zephyr, Stone) charge as you hit. The strong ones need a feat: Volt charges only from volleys, Lumen only from dinks, Frost from winning 6+ shot rallies, Pixel from winning rallies where you volleyed, Bloom from winning rallies while behind, and Nova from a win streak (losing resets it). Strong powers can only be used a few times per match (Bloom and Nova once). Tap the sprite button when its ring is full. Level them up to 5. New sprites only drop when you win: harder modes and tournaments drop rarer ones.
🕹️Arcade is an endless ladder: beat 3 players to 3 points, then a boss to 5 (rally scoring, win by 2), and keep going as it gets harder. Bosses drop Arcade-exclusive characters, paddles, a ball and an arena that you can't get anywhere else.
🐒Orlando has a special SPIN serve: heavy sidespin that bends and kicks off the bounce, so it's much harder to return (small risk of curling wide).
🎄Christmas Drop: from 18 November, 16 festive Locker items (1,500 each) are on sale until New Year's. Buy them and they're yours forever.
👹Krampus Showdown (Christmas event, until New Year's): a super-hard boss, first to 7. Beat him 1, 3, 5 and 10 times for event-only rewards: Lump of Coal ball, Chain Breaker paddle, Krampus Lair arena, and Krampus himself. +250 dinks every win.
🏆Pro Tour mythics (3,000 dinks, Locker): Blaze, Maestro, Rocket and Twister each have a signature serve button and a special shot. Their matching paddles (1,500) make the specials 1.5x stronger.
♿Juan plays by real wheelchair pickleball rules: he may let the ball bounce twice on his side (the second bounce can land anywhere).
🎾Serving: tap the court for a normal serve, or pick SHORT (floats just past the kitchen, pulls them forward) or DEEP (fast to the baseline, harder to return). Both carry a little risk of a fault.
😈CPU sprites: Arcade bosses and Infinite opponents from level 10 bring a sprite too (never in Singles, Doubles or Tour). Watch its red ring top-right: when it fills, they fire a power at you (slow you down, freeze you, shrink your reach, or hit a power shot).
♾️Infinite Arcade has no bosses: just endless games to 5 (each game is one level). Every 15 levels (15, 30, 45…) you can extract and pick an Infinite-only sprite or paddle. The deeper you go, the better your legendary odds, but lose before you extract and you get nothing.
🎲Chaos Arcade is endless games to 5 where every point rolls a random twist, shown under the score: giant ball, tiny or mega paddles, turbo, slow-mo, super bounce, wind, a ghost ball that keeps vanishing, or double points. Lose once and the run ends. Every 5th level cleared gives a Premium Box.
❤️Survival Arcade: one life. Win point after point; lose a single point and the run is over. Every 5 points a new, faster opponent steps in. Streaks of 10, 20 and 35 win a Basic, Premium or Mythic Box.
🎁Dinks come from rallies and games. Spend them in the Locker, open Boxes, and claim your free daily Spin.
