How to play

🕹️Move with the joystick. Touch anywhere on the left half and it appears under your thumb.
🏓Hitting is automatic. Catch the ball on the edge of your paddle to angle it. Near the net you dink, from the back you drive.
⚡LOB lofts your next shot. POWER rips a hard drive (riskier). SPIN curves and dips it, and kicks off the bounce. Paddles with a high SPN stat make it nastier; low-spin paddles make it weak and wild.
📏Rules: serve and return must bounce. No volleys from the kitchen. Only the server scores. Win by 2. Easy games go to 11, Medium to 15, Hard to 21. Same-phone games go to 11.
✨Sprites float beside you in CPU matches. Each gives a passive boost and has a power that charges as you hit. Tap the sprite button when its ring is full. Level them up to 5. New sprites only drop when you win: harder modes and tournaments drop rarer ones.
🕹️Arcade is an endless ladder: beat 3 players to 3 points, then a boss to 5, and keep going as it gets harder. Bosses drop Arcade-exclusive characters, paddles, a ball and an arena that you can't get anywhere else.
😈CPU sprites: on Medium and Hard, Arcade bosses, and Infinite from level 10, your opponent brings a sprite too. Watch its red ring top-right: when it fills, they fire a power at you (slow you down, freeze you, shrink your reach, or hit a power shot).
♾️Infinite Arcade has no bosses: just endless games to 5 (each game is one level). Every 25 levels (25, 50, 75…) you can extract for an Infinite-only sprite. The deeper you go, the better your legendary odds, but lose before you extract and you get nothing.
🎁Dinks come from rallies and games. Spend them in the Locker, open Boxes, and claim your free daily Spin.
